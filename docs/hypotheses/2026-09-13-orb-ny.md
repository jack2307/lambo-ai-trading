# 2026-09-13-orb-ny: a close outside the New York opening range continues in that direction

**Registered:** 2026-09-13 — before any code or run
**Status:** decided → docs/decisions/2026-09-13-orb-ny.md Previously: implemented — `fd-strategy::orb`, 5 tests incl. no-future; in-sample defaults on xauusd:5m: 102 trades, PF 0.897 (a first look, not a result)
**Batch file:** `docs/hypotheses/2026-09-13-orb-ny.toml`

## Claim

The first 30–60 minutes after the COMEX open (08:20 New York) absorb the
overnight order flow and set the day's reference range. A five-minute close
outside that range, taken before midday, continues far enough to pay a stop at
the other side of the range: institutional flow arrives at the open, and a
break of the range it built is that flow winning rather than being absorbed.

## Falsifier

Fails the gate (PF ≥ 1.2, expectancy ≥ 0.05R, ≥ 30 trades) or sits below the
95th percentile of the matched null in-sample; or survives in-sample and fails
on the pre-registered out-of-sample market; or the direction null puts the
side of the break inside a coin flip (< 95th percentile).

## Base method

`NEW: orb` — opening-range breakout.

- Inputs: `rangeStart` (hhmm New York, 0820), `rangeMinutes` (60),
  `entryUntil` (hhmm, 1200), `entryBufferPips` (0), `stopMode` (0 = other side
  of the range, 1 = range midpoint), `riskReward` (1.5), `minRangePips` (5),
  `maxRangeAtr` (3, ATR period `atrPeriod` 14), `pipSize` (0.1).
- Range: high/low of the bars whose New York time is in
  `[rangeStart, rangeStart + rangeMinutes)` on the same New York day; only
  usable once the last of them has closed.
- Entry: the **first** bar of the day, after the range is complete and before
  `entryUntil`, whose close is above `rangeHigh + buffer` (long) or below
  `rangeLow − buffer` (short). One trade per day. Range narrower than
  `minRangePips` or wider than `maxRangeAtr × ATR` → no trade that day.
- Stop: the other side of the range (or its midpoint). Target:
  `riskReward × |entry − stop|` from the close that signalled. Fill is the
  engine's: next bar's open.
- Must not read anything at index > i; the range and the "first breakout"
  test only look back within the day.
- Grid: `riskReward` × `entryBufferPips` = 3 × 3 = 9 cells.

## Data

- In-sample: `xauusd:5m` (1.4 years, Vantage)
- Out-of-sample: `xauduka:5m` (four years, Dukascopy bid, resampled from
  its minutes) — **chosen now, opened only after the in-sample result is
  recorded**
- Costs: `xauusd` config, spread $0.28/oz, swap-free; all hypotheses flat
  16:30–18:15 New York and weekdays only.

## Sample needed

One trade a day at most: ~350 opportunities in-sample, of which a break
happens on perhaps half. Four folds at nine cells need ≥ 60 out-of-sample
trades to say anything; fewer than that is "not enough", not "no".

## What each outcome means

- Survives in-sample AND out-of-sample AND direction null → decision record
  and a paper-run proposal.
- Survives in-sample only → closed; the record says the summer of 2025–26 on
  one feed is what it survived.
- Fails in-sample → closed. No wider grid, no second window.
