## Why a third window

`doji/strict` passed every in-sample condition on 2022–25 (61 trades,
PF 1.203, direction 99th) and was **not confirmed** on 2025–26 (25 trades,
fixed replay PF 0.782, direction 33rd; walk-forward 18 trades PF 1.175).
The confirmation sample is thin, the primary pass is by a hair, and the
confirmation window is the 24%-vol regime that has flattered breaks and
punished fades all night. A window disjoint from both — Dukascopy
2018-06-16 → 2022-06-15 — exists and has not been fetched until now.

This is registered as its own hypothesis so that it cannot be read as a
second bite at the same one: the parameters are **fixed** at the registered
strict preset (`bodyMaxPct 0.05, minRangeAtr 1.5`, grid off), the null is
gated the same way, and there is exactly one row.

## Falsifier

On 2018–22 with fixed parameters: gate (PF ≥ 1.2, expectancy ≥ 0.05R,
≥ 30 trades) and ≥ 95th percentile of the gated null, direction null
≥ 95th. A miss on any of these closes the doji fade; a pass makes it two
disjoint windows out of three, and the record will say the 2025–26 window
was the one it failed.
