# 2026-09-13-doji: a doji at the end of a move marks exhaustion, and the move reverses

**Registered:** (commit time is authoritative) — before any run
**Status:** registered
**Batch file:** `docs/hypotheses/2026-09-13-doji.toml`

## Claim

Asked for by the owner. A bar that travels a full ATR and closes within 10%
of its open, after a directional move of at least 1.5 ATR over ten bars, is
the point where the side that drove the move stopped getting filled. Fading
that bar — stop beyond its far extreme, target 1.5× the risk — captures the
reversal more often than the spread costs.

## Falsifier

Gate and ≥ 95th percentile of random entries gated to the same hours on the
primary window; survives the confirmation; direction null outside (the
direction null is the whole question here: a doji that is *continuation*
would put the fade at the bottom). Both windows.

## Base method

`NEW: doji-reversal` — parameters `bodyMaxPct 0.10`, `minRangeAtr 1.0`,
`trendBars 10`, `trendAtr 1.5`, `bufferPips 3`, `riskReward 1.5`. Grid
`bodyMaxPct` × `riskReward`. First doji of a move only.

## Data

- Gold primary `xauduka:15m` 2022-06 → 2025-04-10; confirmation
  `xauusd:15m` 2025-04-11 → 2026-09-11. Rows: all hours (flat over the
  break), New York hours.
- BTC primary `btc:15m` 2024-08 → 2026-09; confirmation `btcusd:15m`.
- 15-minute bars because that is where the pattern is used; 5-minute dojis
  are noise by construction.

## Sample needed

A doji after a 1.5-ATR move is a few times a week: several hundred signals
on each primary.

## What each outcome means

- Survives both windows and the direction null → record; a candle pattern
  with an edge would be the first.
- Fails primary → closed; the doji is a bar shape, not a forecast.
