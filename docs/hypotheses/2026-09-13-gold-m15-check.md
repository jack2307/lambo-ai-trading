## Claim

Not a new mechanism — a robustness check asked for by the owner: the
opening-range break, the London-range break, and yesterday's-level fade and
break, on 15-minute bars instead of 5-minute. The reason it could differ:
fewer, larger bars mean fewer false closes through a level and a cost that
is a smaller share of each move.

## Falsifier

Same as each original: gate + ≥ 95th percentile of the window-gated null on
the primary, then the confirmation, then direction nulls outside. Both
windows. A pass here on a family closed at 5m would need its own record
and would not reopen the 5m result.

## Base method

`orb` (NY 60m; London 360m) and `pdhl` (fade, break), presets as before.

- Anything less → the 5m records stand and the timeframe is not the variable.
