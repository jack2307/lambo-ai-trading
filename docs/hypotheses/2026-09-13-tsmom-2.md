## Claim

The sign of gold's trailing 20/60/120-day return predicts the sign of its
next weeks' return (Moskowitz, Ooi & Pedersen 2012). Rebalanced at the
first bar after 18:00 New York each day, held until the sign flips, sized
at one percent of equity per two daily ranges, it passes the gate on
2018–25 and on 2025–26 at Vantage cost. Multi-day; admissible because the
account is swap-free (measured). Not an intraday bot. A positive would tell
the volume bot what to condition on, not what to trade.

## Falsifier

Gate (PF ≥ 1.2, expectancy ≥ 0.05R, ≥ 30 trades) and ≥ 95th percentile of
random holds of half the lookback, sized the same way (the drift control),
on the primary with the registered parameters replayed (`fixed = true`);
survives the confirmation; direction null ≥ 95th on the primary. Both
windows. The lookback is pinned per row; nothing is selected.

  (few trades, held for weeks — the opposite of volume).

  Sharpe (0.3–0.5) on one metal over seven years at $0.28/oz is about what a
