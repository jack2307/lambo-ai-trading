# 2026-09-13-gap-fade: the weekend gap on gold fills in the first hours of the week

**Registered:** (commit time is authoritative) — before any run
**Status:** registered
**Batch file:** `docs/hypotheses/2026-09-13-gap-fade.toml`

## Claim

Gold closes Friday 17:00 New York and reopens Sunday 18:00 on thin prints
wherever the weekend's news left it. The open gap is a price nobody
traded through; when the week's liquidity arrives the gap is revisited
more often than not. Fading a gap larger than one ATR back toward the
Friday close, with the stop one gap beyond the open, pays at Vantage cost.

This is a new mechanism for this registry: not a range, not a level inside
the day, not a session — a discontinuity in the price series itself.

## Falsifier

Gate and ≥ 95th percentile of the count-matched null gated to the same
hour (random side, random entry, ATR stop and target), on the primary;
survives the confirmation; direction null ≥ 95th. The engine's maximum
hold is four hours, so the claim is *the gap fills within four hours of
the open*; a gap that needs the week is recorded as not filled. The
registered preset is `gap/1atr`; the walk-forward row selects the grid
and is a check on the preset, not a second bite.

## Base method

`NEW: gap-fade` — on the first bar after a break of ≥ `minGapHours` (24)
in the bars, if |open − last close| ≥ `minGapAtr` × ATR (ATR at the last
bar before the break), enter toward the last close; target the close
(`fillPct` 100), stop `stopGapMult` gaps beyond the open. One trade per
gap. `Exits::Engine`.

## Data

- Primary: `xauduka:5m` bounded `2018-06-16 → 2025-04-10` (five-minute bars
  so the gap bar is the open itself).
- Confirmation: `xauusd:5m` from `2025-04-11`.
- Entries gated to 17:00–19:00 New York (the reopen in either DST state);
  the null enters in the same window on any day, count-matched.

## Sample needed

Seven years is ~350 weekends; at one ATR perhaps a third of them gap that
far — 100+ trades on the primary. The confirmation has ~70 weekends.

## What each outcome means

- Survives both → record; a Sunday-open paper run. One trade a week is not a
  volume bot, and the record will say so.
- Survives the primary only → regime; closed.
- Fails the primary → closed; the gap is either filled by the first prints
  (before a five-minute bar can act) or not a gap that reverts.
