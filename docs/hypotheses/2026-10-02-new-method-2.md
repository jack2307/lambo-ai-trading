# 2026-10-02-new-method-2 — why the first-window clocks and trend signs died on the second window

**Registered:** 2026-10-02, before any run. Nothing below is computed yet.
**Branch:** `agent/new-method-2`. Worktree `E:/rust/fd-wt-nm2`.
**Status:** open.

## The question

Four closed registrations from 2026-09-13/14 measured an effect at the 95th
percentile or better of their own nulls on a FIRST window and then read a
SECOND window. Nobody has answered **why the second window disagreed**. There
are four candidate answers and they point in opposite directions:

- **(a) never real** — the first window was selection over many tried rows;
- **(b) real and decayed** — the behaviour changed at a locatable time;
- **(c) real and still there, but the second window's TEST differed** — cost,
  session boundary, clock offset, bar source, or corrupt records;
- **(d) the second window could not have detected it** — too small.

(a) says stop. (d) says the test never answered. They must be separated with
numbers, not a preference.

## The four claims under examination

Taken from the closed records, not recomputed here:

| id | claim | first window | second window |
|---|---|---|---|
| `fri` | gold long Friday 16:30 NY -> Sunday 18:30 (`2026-09-13-friday-weekend-hold`, `2026-09-13-close-reopen-drift`) | 2018-06 -> 2025-04, 336 holds, PF 2.064, 100th | 2010-06 -> 2018-06, 400 holds, PF 0.983, 92nd |
| `eu` | EURUSD short 03:00 -> 11:00 NY (`2026-09-14-fx-local-hours`, `-sign`) | 2010-06 -> 2018-06, 2,087/2,088 holds, 99th, excess -2.84 pips/hold, t = -3.18 | 2018-06 -> 2026-05, 2,056/2,057 holds, 67th, excess -0.10 pips/hold, t = -0.16 |
| `xag20` | silver 20-day sign, rebalanced 16:15 NY (`2026-09-13-tsmom-silver`) | 2010-06 -> 2018-06, 189 trades, PF 1.341, 100th/98th | 2018-06 -> 2026-05, 186 trades, PF 1.026, 63rd/62nd |
| `close` | gold long 16:30 -> 18:15 NY weekdays (`2026-09-13-close-reopen-drift`) | 2018-06 -> 2025-04, 1,355 holds, PF 1.254, 100th | 2025-04 -> 2026-09, 282 holds, PF 1.299, 96th |

`close` is carried as the **control that did not die**, because an explanation
that cannot tell it apart from the other three explains nothing.

## What will be measured, and nothing else

**M1 — multiplicity, registered against tried.** A plain count.
*Registered:* for each registration in the 2026-09-12 -> 2026-09-18 programme,
the multiplicity its own text declares (searching its registration and decision
for a declared count, a Bonferroni correction, or a statement of degrees of
freedom). *Tried:* the number of distinct method rows that a receipt in
`docs/research/runs/` prints a percentile for, counted once per
(run directory, row id) pair, over the same dates. Both numbers published with
their counting rule; neither adjusted after it is seen.

**M2 — power of the second window, for each of the four claims.** The second
window's own null distribution, as published in its receipt, supplies the
sampling spread under no effect: sigma = (null p95 − null p50) / 1.645, in
profit factor. Power = Phi( (theta_1 − p50_2)/sigma_2 − 1.645 ), one-sided
alpha = 0.05, where theta_1 is the first window's own point estimate. For `eu`
the same quantity is also computed in the units the registration declared —
pips of detrended excess per hold — from the published per-hold standard
deviations (40.8 and 27.5 pips) and counts, two-sided alpha = 0.05, so two
independent routes answer the same question. The smallest effect the second
window could have detected at 0.80 power is reported for every claim, in the
claim's own units.

**M3 — first-window p-values against the true tried count.** For each claim the
first window's one-sided p-value from its own null (percentile, or the
registration's published t where the record quotes one), multiplied by M1's
tried count, by Holm's ordering as well as Bonferroni.

**M4 — the (c) instruments, all read-only.**
1. **Bar source.** `data/bars/XAUDUKA-15m.parquet` against its `.bak`: first and
   last bar of each, the merge seam, bars per weekday per year, coverage.
   Whether `fri`'s two windows are the same file segment.
2. **Session boundary.** Count of bars in the 17:00–17:59 New York bucket per
   year, on `XAUDUKA`, `XAGDUKA`, `EURDUKA` and `XAUUSD`. The record states the
   break is absent before 2013; this measures it.
3. **Cost in proportional terms.** 0.28 price units as basis points of the mean
   price per year, 2010 -> 2026. A constant absolute spread is not a constant
   test when the underlying triples.
4. **Clock shift scan.** The `eu` window and the `fri`/`close` windows shifted
   by −8 ... +8 whole hours, including exactly ±7 (the OTL `utc_offset_hours = 7`
   trap of `docs/decisions/2026-09-12-otl-timestamps.md`), on BOTH windows of
   each claim. The configured offset is not removed anywhere; this is a scan
   over the measurement window, not a change to the parser. A shift that
   recovers the first window's effect size inside the second window is a
   finding; the scan's maximum is otherwise not quoted as a result.
5. **Corrupt records.** `hold_ms < 0` rows in `data/paper/*/trades.jsonl`,
   counted; label-against-own-R contradictions counted; and the date range of
   the corrupt rows set against the four claims' windows, to say whether the
   defect can touch this comparison at all.

**M5 — the drift control, applied before any of the above is believed.** Gold's
unconditional drift is +0.3946 ATR20 per 5 sessions (t = 6.74) and neither null
in this repository controls for it, so any one-sided long row beats both nulls
for free. For every claim: the long/short split of entries, the same measurement
with sides flipped, and — the test the repository lacks — the hold's return
minus the same period's own move pro-rated to the hold's length, which is the
detrending `scripts/fx_window_drift.py` already applies to `eu` and nothing
applies to `fri` or `close`. If a one-sided claim's first-window effect does not
survive that detrending, the claim is the drift and is reported as the drift
whatever its percentile was.

## Decision rule, fixed now

Per claim, one letter:

- **(d) ruled out** if M2 power >= 0.80. **(d) supported** if power <= 0.50.
  Power in (0.50, 0.80) is reported as **unresolved between (d) and the rest**
  and named as such; no letter is assigned.
- **(a) supported** if M3's corrected first-window p-value > 0.05 at the tried
  count. **(a) ruled out** if it stays <= 0.05 at the tried count.
- **(c) supported** if any M4 instrument differs between the claim's two windows
  by enough to move the measured effect at least half of the first window's
  point estimate. **(c) ruled out** only when all five instruments are read and
  none does.
- **(b) supported** only if (a), (c) and (d) are all ruled out AND M5's
  detrended yearly series locates the change — a year, named, with the
  cumulative sum shown.
- If two letters remain, **say which two and the one measurement that would
  separate them.** A narrowing is an acceptable outcome; a preference is not.

## Amendment, 2026-10-02 (afternoon): the stricter test, registered before it runs

The letters are assigned and the deliverable carries them. The one claim that did
**not** die — `close`, gold long 16:30 -> 18:30 New York, Monday to Thursday — is
also the only one whose drift-detrended excess is positive on two independent
eight-year windows. Its second window's power is 0.486, below the 0.50 floor this
registration set, so **(d) is supported for it** and nothing about it is settled by
the 2026-09-13 record. And the cost it was charged, 0.28 price units, is 2.21
basis points of the gold price in 2018 and 0.58 in 2026, so **(c) is live for it
too**: the two windows were not priced the same way in any proportional sense.

Those two letters imply one test, and it is registered here before it runs.

### The question

Is the gold close-to-reopen hold a **fixed absolute** effect in dollars an ounce,
or a **fixed proportional** effect in basis points of price? If it is proportional
and constant, then the row's apparent arrival after 2023 is the absolute cost
shrinking against a tripled price, and the row never became a method — it only
stopped being obviously impossible. If the proportional excess itself grew, the
market changed and (c) is refuted for this claim.

### What is measured, and nothing else

The row exactly as `2026-09-13-close-reopen-drift` registered it: `session-hold`,
LONG, entry at the open of the first bar at or after 16:30 New York, exit at the
open of the first bar at or after 18:15, `weekdays:MoTuWeTh`. No parameter moved.
Three windows, each measured twice — once in **dollars an ounce** and once in
**basis points of the hold's own entry price**:

1. `xauduka` 2010-06-01 -> 2018-06-15
2. `xauduka` 2018-06-16 -> 2025-04-10
3. `xauusd` 2025-04-11 -> 2026-09-11

Each hold's excess is its raw move minus the window's own unconditional drift
multiplied by the hold's length in hours — the drift control neither null in this
repository applies. Cost is 0.28 price units a round turn, converted to basis
points of the same entry price.

### Falsifiers, with numbers, fixed now

- **F1, the (c) claim.** If the three windows' **basis-point** excesses are
  mutually consistent — every one inside 2 standard errors of their
  inverse-variance weighted mean, and a chi-square across the three with 2
  degrees of freedom under 5.99 — while the **dollar** excesses are not (chi-square
  above 5.99), then the difference between the windows is the price level and not
  the market, and (c) is confirmed for `close`. If the basis-point excesses are
  themselves heterogeneous, F1 fails and (c) is refuted for this claim.
- **F2, the row itself.** The row is a method on a window only if its
  basis-point excess **net of the basis-point cost** is positive with t >= 2.0 on
  that window. Measured on all three. A row that clears F2 on the newest window
  alone and not on the two eight-year windows is not a method; it is a cost
  curve.
- **F3, the drift.** The row is 100% long by construction. The same figures with
  the side flipped are reported. If the detrended excess does not keep its sign
  and magnitude under detrending, the row is the drift and F2 is not consulted.

### Multiplicity

3 windows x 1 row x 2 units = **6 cells, all reported**, none selected. No
threshold moved, no filter added, no parameter fitted. The 17-cell shift scan
above is not part of this test and its maximum is not quoted as a result.

**No outcome of this test is a paper run, a strategy or a promotion**, including
an outcome in which the row clears F2 on all three windows. `data-sealed/` is not
read. If the answer needs an unspent window, this registration says so and stops.
