## Claim

Time-series momentum — hold the side of the asset's own trailing return —
has out-of-sample evidence across 58 futures markets over a century,
including gold. Rebalanced daily at the New York open (18:00), it should
pass the gate on gold 2022–25 and on BTC 2024–26. This is a *multi-day*
holding claim; it is admissible here because the account is swap-free and
the loop's cost model charges nothing overnight (measured). Not an
intraday bot; registered because it is the one mechanism with a
literature the night has not touched, and because a positive would change
what the volume bot should be conditioned on.

## Falsifier

Gate and ≥ 95th percentile of random holds of the same length (the drift
control), on the primary; survives the confirmation; direction null outside.
Both windows. The grid is the lookback only (20/60/120 days).

## Base method

`NEW: tsmom` — at the first bar at or after 18:00 New York each day, hold
the sign of the return over `lookbackDays`; flip on a sign change, else stay.
`Exits::Strategy`, no stop.

## Data

- Gold: primary `xauduka:5m` 2022-06 → 2025-04-10; confirmation `xauusd:5m`
  2025-04 → 2026-09.
- BTC: primary `btc:5m` 2024-08 → 2026-09 (two years, short for a
  60-day lookback: ~10 independent bets); confirmation `btcusd:5m`.
- No session filters; weekdays only for gold's rebalance to land on bars.

## Sample needed

Holds last weeks; 2022–25 gives perhaps 20–40 sign changes at 60 days.
Thin. The gate's 30-trade floor will bind; a fail on trade count is "not
enough", not "no".

## What each outcome means

- Survives both windows on gold → record; a swing paper run, and a real
  question for the IB model (few trades, held days — the opposite of volume).
- Survives primary only → regime; closed.
- Fails primary → closed; the century-long effect does not show on three
  years of one metal at this cost, which is also what the literature's
  Sharpe (~0.3–0.5) would predict for a sample this short.
