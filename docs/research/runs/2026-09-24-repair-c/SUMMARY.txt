At 168 h the weekend flat at 16:40 New York and the news flat at 60/30 close
nearly half of all positions and TIMEOUT closes four. **The cap stops binding
somewhere between 24 h and 72 h; past that the guards are the horizon.** Mean
hold on struct-80 is 1,887 minutes at a 10,080-minute cap — 31 hours under a
one-week ceiling — because a hold cannot cross a weekend.

Lifting the cap past about 72 hours therefore buys almost nothing on a guarded
run, and that is a statement about the guards, not about the market.

## 7. Bar counting, checked rather than assumed

`max_hold_ms` is wall-clock milliseconds and nothing in this measurement
resampled or counted bars. The one place a bar width enters is the weekend
guard's `bar_interval_ms`, which takes the **minimum** positive gap between
consecutive bars and so returns 15 minutes on a gapped 15m series exactly as on
an ungapped one. The 2013 Dukascopy metals bar-rule change (longest consecutive
run 476 before, exactly 92 after) is not exposed here in any case: every run
above is Vantage XAUUSD from 2022-06-16, not XAUDUKA.


## 8. Caveats, and defects found — including in what I did

**8.1 The two families disagree, and both readings are real.** Family 2's three
verdict changes are on the walk-forward path, which selects parameters per fold
from the preset's grid. Family 1 holds the registered parameters fixed over the
whole window and **no verdict changes there at any horizon**: struct-80's
whole-window PF at 72 h is 1.129, below the 1.20 gate, while its walk-forward
out-of-sample PF is 1.210, above it. CLAUDE.md says to quote the walk-forward, so
the honest headline is "the cap changed a verdict"; the honest small print is that
at fixed registered parameters over the whole window it did not, and that the
difference is per-fold selection, not the horizon.

**8.2 The direction percentile is a percentile of a different quantity.** In
`rescore_hypothesis`, `matched_pct_*` is the percentile of the **walk-forward
out-of-sample** PF inside a walk-forward null, while `dir_pct_*` is the percentile
of the **whole-window** PF inside a whole-window null (hypotheses.rs:951-954; the
struct's own doc comment says so). `passes_net()` therefore requires the gate and
the matched leg on one basis and the direction leg on another. It is documented in
the code and NOT in the printed table, so a reader of the receipt cannot tell. Not
introduced here; recorded because this task's verdict changes rest on it.

**8.3 The rescore receipt has no expectancy column.** A row that passes prints no
expectancy anywhere, so the exact figure for the three passing rows is not
recoverable from the receipt — only the fact that it is at or above 0.05 R. Family
1's table carries expectancy at every horizon and is the one to read for it.

**8.4 Lifting the cap changes how well the matched null is count-matched.** This
is an interaction nobody has recorded. On the structural arm the count match
IMPROVES with the horizon (struct-20: 0.80 -> 0.92 -> 0.97 -> 0.98 in Family 1)
because the method's own trade count falls toward the control's. On the ATR arm it
DEGRADES (atr-20: 0.67 -> 0.57; in Family 2, 0.60 -> 0.42) because the ATR arm's
count barely moves while the control's falls. So the horizon a row is read at
partly determines whether its percentile is the method's size at all. Three of the
eight rows are out of band at 4 h and four are out of band at 168 h.

**8.5 The change's own limitation.** A horizon can only be stated by a config
directory; there is no `--max-hold=` flag. That is deliberate — a horizon is a
declared property of a market, not an ad-hoc flag, and a flag would let a run
change the clock without a config anyone can diff — but it means every receipt
here depends on a `local.toml` beside a copy of `config/`. The mitigation is that
`search` now prints the resolved value AND its source on every run, so a receipt
says which horizon it measured whether or not the config dir still exists. The
three `local-h*.toml` files in this directory are the exact overrides used.

**8.6 What was NOT measured.** `[trading] reward_risk` is still global with no
per-market override, so "a stop and no target" remains inexpressible under
`Exits::Engine` (see `crates/fd-backtest/tests/max_hold_horizon.rs`,
`a_no_target_entry_is_given_one`). I did not add a second override for it: that is
a separate change with its own blast radius and it is not this task. No horizon
was measured on any market other than xauusd, on any instrument other than the
Vantage 15m bars, or past 2025-09-23.
