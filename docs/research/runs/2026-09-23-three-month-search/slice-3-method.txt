Mechanisms in this slice, five of them, with their full registry grids:

Tool: crates/fd-backtest/src/bin/three_month_slice3.rs, new in this commit. It
exists because no existing mode ranks individual cells by return and then
replays the named cells on a second window without re-fitting them; --mode=wf
reports one number per mechanism and --mode=hypotheses needs a batch file whose
rows are known in advance. It chooses no threshold of its own: the gate, the
fold count, select_by and the spread all come from config/default.toml, and the
three survival legs come from the registration.

Commands, exactly as run:

--to is exclusive, which is why the in-sample call names 2026-09-24 and the
out-of-sample call names 2026-06-23. The two windows are contiguous and do not
overlap by a single bar.

WHAT THE WINDOW ACTUALLY WAS. The registration names 2026-06-23 -> 2026-09-23.
The 15m store's last bar is 2026-09-17 13:00, because py/ingest/mt5_export.py
has not been run since 17 September, so the in-sample window is
2026-06-23 00:00 -> 2026-09-17 13:00: 5,731 bars, about 86 days rather than 92.
Nothing was done about this — extending the store would change the data under a
registration that is already committed — and it is recorded here rather than
silently absorbed. The out-of-sample window is the full
2025-09-23 00:00 -> 2026-06-22 23:45, 17,577 bars.

HOW A CELL WAS SCORED IN SAMPLE, and the one place this is an interpretation
rather than an instruction. The slice's assignment says "walk-forward with the
existing fold count ... Rank every cell by return over the window." A fixed
cell has nothing to select, so a per-cell number cannot be a walk-forward
number: the walk-forward is a property of the mechanism, not of a cell. Both are
therefore reported and they are labelled differently. The ranking that decides
the top five is each cell's return_pct over the whole in-sample window, guards
on, spread 0.28 — an in-sample number, which is what the registration calls it.
Beside it, slice-3-in-sample.txt reports each mechanism's own walk-forward over
the same window at the configured four folds and select_by = expectancy, fold by
fold, with the parameters each fold selected. If the other three agents read
that sentence the other way their in-sample columns will not be comparable to
this one, and that is the reason it is written down here.

No filters were applied to the cells. The registration says guards on, the
configured spread and the registry's own grids, and names no filter; the
rebate-rescore batch's rows carry ["weekdays", "flat:1630-1815"] but that is
that batch's screen and not this one's. The consequence is reported in the
result rather than assumed: gap-fade is scoreable here, at 5 to 9 trades in
three months, where under the screen's weekdays filter its Sunday-reopen entry
is removed entirely. The guards were on throughout and the header line of each
receipt names them.

HOW THE OUT-OF-SAMPLE LEG WAS SCORED. Each of the five cells was replayed
through fd_backtest::hypotheses::run_hypothesis_fixed_guarded — the desk's
existing replay path, no folds, no selection, the parameters named in
slice-3-cells.txt and nothing else — against 200 matched-null seeds. The three
legs are the registration's: expectancy > 0, profit factor >= 1.2, and the 95th
percentile of the matched null. None of the three was moved, and neither was the
spread. The count match is printed on every row so the percentile can be
checked rather than trusted; all five landed at 1.08 or 1.09, inside the 0.25
band that docs/decisions/2026-09-23-matched-null-repair.md set.

DEVIATIONS, all of them.

1. The registration carries the top five overall forward. Splitting the search
   across four agents carries five per slice, so twenty in total. That is more

   result; it is recorded because it is a deviation.
2. The in-sample window is six days shorter than the registration names, for the
   data reason above.
3. The per-cell in-sample ranking is a plain in-sample return, not a
   walk-forward return, for the reason above. The walk-forward is reported

RESTATEMENT, 2026-09-23, AFTER THE FIRST NUMBERS HAD BEEN SEEN

This is the weak part of the record and it is stated first rather than buried.
The ranking above was run, its top five were carried out of sample, and all of
those numbers were read, BEFORE the ranking was changed. The coordinator then
reconciled slice 3 against slices 1 and 2, which had both ranked by walk-forward
return, and against the registration's own wording — "the winner is whichever
cell has the highest walk-forward return on the three-month window, chosen by the
existing select_by rule" — and ruled that this slice's reading had to move. So
the restated column is not what was planned here; it is a correction made with
the first answer already in hand, and anyone reading these receipts should weight
it accordingly. Both columns are published side by side in
slice-3-in-sample-restated.txt for exactly that reason, and the original
slice-3-in-sample.txt and slice-3-cells.txt are left untouched as the first
version rather than overwritten.

What the restatement does. Every one of the forty-five cells is wrapped in
fd_backtest::hypotheses::Preset::new over BOTH of its grid axes, which empties
the preset's grid, so walk_forward_guarded has exactly one combination to choose
from in each of the four folds and measures that cell on each fold's test window
instead of selecting a different cell. A cell's walk-forward return is the
return_pct of the combined out-of-sample folds. Nothing else changed: same
window, same guards, same 0.28 spread, same four folds, same
select_by = expectancy, same five-training-trade floor per fold, and the six-day
feed shortfall stays exactly as recorded above. No threshold moved and no filter
was loosened.

A number this ranking needs read with it. A cell whose training windows never
reach the five-trade floor has NO walk-forward number, which is not the same as a
walk-forward return of zero, so such cells are printed as "no fold" and sorted
below every cell that has one. And the folds column matters as much as the
return: four of the five cells this ranking promotes have ONE measured fold and
TWO walk-forward trades, with an infinite profit factor because neither trade
lost. Their walk-forward returns of 1.14% and 0.57% rest on two trades each.
That is a property of the registered ranking on mechanisms this thin, not a
finding, and it is why the out-of-sample leg is the only part of this slice that
carries weight.

The top five DID change, so the out-of-sample leg was re-run on the new five:
slice-3-cells-restated.txt and slice-3-out-of-sample-restated.txt, same three
legs, same 200 seeds, achieved count match printed per row (1.05 to 1.08, all
inside the band). Nought of five cleared all three legs, as before. One row wants
a note: doji-reversal bodyMaxPct=0.05,riskReward=2 shows a positive nine-month
return of 1.48% against a NEGATIVE expectancy of -0.069R and a total of -5.31R.
The guards sized 65 of its 77 entries down, so its dollar return and its R
arithmetic disagree, and the registration's legs are expectancy and profit
factor rather than return precisely so that a row like this cannot pass on the
dollar column alone. It fails on all three legs regardless, at the 65th
percentile of its matched null.

The original out-of-sample receipt, slice-3-out-of-sample.txt, remains valid for
the five cells it names; those five were the top five under the superseded
ranking and their figures are unchanged by anything here.
