WHAT WAS SWEPT. Twenty-six cells, where a cell is one point of a mechanism's
own registry grid with every swept axis pinned to it: nine for trend-pullback
(fast x riskReward), nine for volume-thrust (volMult x riskReward), three for
volman-box (boxBars), three for tsmom (lookbackDays), and one each for
intraday-momentum and session-hold, which declare no grid at all. Twenty-six is
N, and the best of twenty-six is what the ranked table reports. The window is
2026-06-23 to 2026-09-23 as registered, but the stored 15m bars end on
2026-09-17 13:00Z, so 5,731 bars ran and the real window is a week short of
three months. Guards on, configured spread 0.28, four folds, select_by
expectancy, min_trades_per_cell 5, all from config; no threshold was touched.

THE IN-SAMPLE NUMBER IS NOT SPECTACULAR, AND THAT IS ALSO A RESULT. The
registration pre-committed to publishing a flattering number as a flattering
number. There is none to publish in this slice. The best walk-forward return of
twenty-six cells is session-hold at +1.63%, then intraday-momentum at +0.11%,
and every one of the remaining nine cells that traded at all is negative, down
to -8.96%. The best whole-window replay of any cell is volume-thrust at
volMult 2, riskReward 1, which returns +2.02% on four trades. The owner asked
for at least 50% over three months. On these six mechanisms, at 1% risk with
the configured costs, the search cannot manufacture even a lucky 5%, which is
the registration's own last branch: the window is too short at this risk level
to produce a flattering number.

FIFTEEN OF THE TWENTY-SIX CELLS ARE UNSCOREABLE AND NO FILTER WAS LOOSENED TO
FIX THAT. Every tsmom cell took no trade at all over the three months at any
lookback: tsmom rebalances daily but holds for weeks off 15m bars, and the
warmup alone is longer than the window. Every volman-box cell took at most one
trade over the whole window and none inside a fold. Every volume-thrust cell
took between zero and four trades over the whole window and none inside a fold.
A fold needs five training trades to select anything, so those fifteen cells
produced no walk-forward book, and a cell that took no trade has no return.
They are ranked last rather than at a fabricated 0.00%, which would have put
every mechanism that never fired above every mechanism that fired and lost, and
they are listed with their counts at the foot of the receipt.

THE CARRIED FIVE. Ranks 1 to 5 of the eleven cells that produced a book:
session-hold, intraday-momentum, trend-pullback at fast 10 / riskReward 2,
trend-pullback at fast 34 / riskReward 1, and trend-pullback at fast 10 /
riskReward 1.5. They were replayed on 2025-09-23 to 2026-06-22, 17,485 bars,
at those parameters with no folds and no selection, so nothing was re-fitted.

None of the five survived. Every one fails the first leg and the second on its
own: expectancy is negative on all five and no profit factor reaches 1.2, so
the third leg never decides anything. The two cells that looked positive in
sample lose 14.94% and 0.36% on the nine months they were not selected on. The
answer to the owner's question is the one the registration expected: a
three-month search on these mechanisms buys a number, and the number does not
survive contact with the nine months before it.

THE HOLD NULL IS REPORTED AS UNMEASURED, NOT QUOTED. session-hold and
intraday-momentum manage their own exits, so hypotheses.rs reads them against
RandomHold rather than random entries, and that control is not count-matched;
the repair of 2026-09-23 did not touch it and says so. The controls took a
median of 1,289 and 2,722 trades against these cells' 191 and 179, ratios of
6.75 and 15.20. The existing printer calls those rows the 12th and the 52nd
percentile and flags them; this receipt prints UNMEASURED with the ratio
beside it instead, because a percentile read against a control fifteen times
the method's size is not the method's percentile. The gate and expectancy legs
are unaffected and both fail, so nothing turns on it here.

AND TSMOM WOULD HAVE BEEN A THIRD SUCH CASE. The brief named two mechanisms as
hold-null cases. Pinning a cell makes it three: tsmom also has a self-managed
exit, and hypotheses.rs takes the hold-null path when the exit is self-managed
AND the preset's grid is empty, which pinning lookbackDays makes true. It never
reached the out-of-sample leg because it took no trade in sample, so no such
row appears here, but any future cell-level run of tsmom will carry the same
UNMEASURED handling and for the same reason.

THE DEFECT FOUND IN MY OWN RUNNER, PUBLISHED BESIDE ITS REPAIR. The first pair
of receipts was produced without installing the news calendar, so the news flat
in the guards line (60/30 minutes, impact 3 and above, USD) was inert while the
header said guards were on. The cross-check against the existing search binary,
which does install it, is what caught it: 917 trades against 910 on one cell,
profit factors apart in the second decimal, and NEWS_FLAT refusals in one run
and none in the other. It also changed which cell was rank 5 in sample, from
fast 34 / riskReward 2 to fast 10 / riskReward 1.5, so the carried five are not
the same five. The defective receipts are kept under
*-without-news-calendar.txt rather than deleted. After the fix every column the
two printers share is identical on all five cells.

DEVIATION ON THE RECORD. The registration carries the top five overall forward.
Splitting the sweep across four agents means twenty cells are carried, five per
slice, which is more out-of-sample tests and not fewer, so it cannot flatter
the result. Recorded here as a deviation all the same.
