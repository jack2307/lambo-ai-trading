WHAT WAS SWEPT

Forty-five cells: nine for each of the five mechanisms, which is each
mechanism's own grid in the registry, unchanged, with no filters added. Both
axes of every grid were pinned per cell through Preset::new, so the
walk-forward had nothing left to select and measured that one parameter set on
each of the four folds' test blocks. Guards on, configured spread 0.28,
starting equity 100 (the cent account's 10,000 USC), select_by expectancy, four
folds, minimum five trades per training cell. Forty-five is the N behind "the
best of N" for this slice; three other agents swept their own.

THE WINDOW IS SHORT OF WHAT WAS REGISTERED

The registration asks for 2026-06-23 → 2026-09-23. The XAUUSD 15m store ends at
2026-09-17 13:00, so the in-sample window is 5,731 bars covering 2026-06-23 →
2026-09-17: six calendar days short at the near end, about 93% of the window
asked for. The nine-month window is fully covered, 17,485 bars from
2025-09-23 00:00 to 2026-06-21 23:45. Nothing was done about the shortfall
because extending the feed would have meant touching the MT5 export mid-run;
it is recorded instead. A shorter search window produces, if anything, a less
impressive in-sample number, so it does not flatter what follows.

THE TWO MECHANISMS ON THE FUNDED ACCOUNT, SAID SEPARATELY

macd-cross runs live as xau-macd-asia and stoch-reversal as xau-stoch. Neither
live row is a cell of this sweep: both run at default parameters with filters
(weekdays, NY 18:00-02:00 and news 60-30 for the first; weekdays and news
60-30 for the second), while the sweep runs the bare grids all day. So they
were measured again in their own configuration on the same three months, and
that is slice-2-funded-rows.txt.

xau-macd-asia on the three months: 97 trades, profit factor 1.134, expectancy
+0.031R, 76th percentile of a null that matched at 0.93. The record's headline
for this row is profit factor 1.56 on 300 trades at the 100th percentile
matched and the 93rd on direction, from the recent-year screen. The three
months do not reproduce that: the profit factor is 1.134, not 1.56, and the
percentile is the 76th, not the 100th. It is still positive and it is still the
best-behaved mechanism in this slice, but the number on the window the owner
asked about is materially below what the record advertises, and it does not
clear the gate on this window. That is the record's own three-year figure of
0.91 looking more representative than its one-year figure.

There is a second reading in the other direction and it belongs here too. The
registration quotes the best expectancy this desk has ever measured as +0.011R,
for macd-cross over 923 closed trades. The unfiltered macd-cross cell
fast=16 riskReward=2 returns +0.049R over 862 trades on the nine months — about
four and a half times the record's headline figure, on a comparable number of
trades. The verdict does not change, because 1.033 is not 1.2 and the 74th is
not the 95th, but the record's "best ever +0.011R" is not the ceiling it reads
as, and a reader of that line should know an all-day variant measured higher on
a window the line does not cover.

xau-stoch on the three months: 384 trades, profit factor 0.836, expectancy
-0.087R, 14th percentile of its null (count match 0.69, so that percentile is
unmatched, again in the conservative direction). Its entry on the candidate
list was honest about being in-sample only — profit factor 1.48 on 35 trades of
one week — and the nearest matched-null figure for the mechanism was the 30th
percentile. The three months are worse than "inside the noise": the mechanism
is below the median of random entry over nearly four hundred trades, and the
unfiltered sweep agrees emphatically, all nine grid cells losing over the whole
window, the worst at -28.23%. This does not contradict the
record, which never claimed an edge for it; it contradicts the idea that live
money should keep finding out. Saying so is not a promotion or demotion
decision, which is not this registration's to make.

DEVIATION FROM THE REGISTRATION, RECORDED

The registration says the top five cells overall are carried to the nine
months. The search was split across four agents by mechanism, so each carried
its own top five and twenty cells were tested out of sample rather than five.
That is more out-of-sample tests, not fewer, and it cannot flatter the result;
it is recorded because it is not what was written down.

Nothing from this run goes anywhere near the funded account, per the
registration's third reporting commitment. No threshold was moved: the gate is
the config's 30 / 1.2 / 0.05, both null readings stayed at the 95th, and the
spread stayed at the configured 0.28.
