# VWAP fade on BTC: after a stretch, price keeps going

**Date:** 2026-09-13
**Question:** Does BTC, having closed more than 1.5 ATRs from the day's VWAP,
return toward it more often than not? (`docs/hypotheses/2026-09-13-vwap-fade.md`,
registered at `4510e92`.)
**Outcome:** No. Binance 5m, 2024-08 → 2026-09: PF 0.951 on 5,828 trades all
day (44th percentile of random entries), 0.917 in US hours (24th). The
direction null puts the fade at the **13th–15th percentile** — the opposite
side would have been in the 85th–87th, still inside — so if anything a
stretch continues. Confirmation not opened. Closed.

## Evidence

vwap/allday           5828   0.951  -0.029    0.957    1.021   44%  fail

vwap/wide             5828   0.951  -0.029    0.957    1.021   44%  fail  (see note)

Direction nulls: allday 0.922 → 13th; us-hours 0.942 → 15th; wide 0.969 →
77th (its defaults were the registered 2.5-ATR stretch, which the
walk-forward rows did not honour — see note).

**Note on `vwap/wide`.** Its rows are identical to `allday` because the
walk-forward grid sweeps `stretchAtr` and the grid replaced the preset. A
preset on a gridded parameter was silently ignored; from this pass a preset
*pins* the parameter and removes it from the grid (`Preset::grid`). The
direction null, which uses the preset directly, is the only receipt that
tested 2.5 ATR: 77th, inside.

## Reading

The named counterparty — desks benchmarked to VWAP — either is not there
at five-minute horizons or is outweighed by whatever produced the stretch.
On BTC in this window a 1.5-ATR dislocation is more often the start of a
move than its end, but not reliably enough to trade the other way either
(the flipped side is inside its null too). Mean reversion to the session
average at this timeframe and cost has no edge.

## What each role said

Reviews not spawned: all rows fail the gate, the direction null is the
sharpest reading and it points away from the claim. The receipts are the
review.

## What would reopen this

A VWAP anchored to the New York day rather than the UTC day, *and* a reason
to think the five-minute horizon is where the benchmarked flow acts — the
literature has it at the daily horizon, where this project does not trade.

## What this does not say

- It does not say momentum after a stretch is an edge: the flipped side is
  inside the null.
- It does not say anything about gold: Dukascopy has no volume, and the
  claim needs it.
