# New York opening-range breakout: above an all-day null, inside its own, below the gate

**Date:** 2026-09-13
**Question:** Does a five-minute close outside the 08:20–09:20 (or 08:50) New
York opening range, before midday, continue far enough to pay a stop at the
other side of the range? (`docs/hypotheses/2026-09-13-orb-ny.md`, pre-registered
at commit `5625809`.)
**Outcome:** No. Two of three variants passed the gate in-sample against the
null as first specified, then failed the gate on the pre-registered four-year
market (PF 0.95–1.12). The adversary showed the first null was mis-matched;
against a null gated to the strategy's own window the survivors are at the
92nd/86th percentile in-sample and 72nd/96th out of sample. The direction
null is a coin flip on both feeds (34th, 54th). Closed. This was the first
pass of the `/research` loop end to end.

## What was measured

- Method: `fd-strategy::orb`, written from the spec after registration. Five
  unit tests including one that alters every bar after `i` and asserts the
  signal is unchanged. Data-integrity confirmed no look-ahead
  (`orb.rs` reads `ctx.bars[..=ctx.i]`; first-break test walks `bars[..ctx.i]`).
- In-sample `xauusd:5m` (Vantage, 2025-04-11 → 2026-09-11); out-of-sample
  `xauduka:5m` (Dukascopy bid resampled from minutes, 2022-06-16 → 2026-05-31).
  Costs: spread $0.28/oz, swap-free, flat 16:30–18:15 New York, weekdays.
- Walk-forward 4 folds, grid `riskReward` × `entryBufferPips` (9 cells), 200
  matched-null seeds; direction null 1000 assignments on each market.
- Receipts: `docs/research/runs/2026-09-13-orb-ny/` and, for the corrected
  control, `docs/research/runs/2026-09-13-orb-ny-matched-null/`.

## Reading

The 100th percentiles were real and meant something other than what they
looked like: the registered null traded random bars across the whole day at
ATR stops, and a method that trades only the New York morning beats an
all-day random entry on cost alone. Gated to the same window the survivors
are inside the noise. Out of sample the gate fails on every row, the in-sample
ranking inverts (60m best in, 30m best out), and the side of the break is a
coin flip on 341 trades across both feeds. What survives is "the New York
morning is not the worst time to be in gold", which is not a strategy.

Two things about the *sample*, from the review, that make the fail
conservative: 28% of the out-of-sample range-window days are the in-sample
price series (the feeds overlap 2025-04-11 → 2026-05-31 at correlation
0.983), and the survivors still failed; and `orb/60m-expansion` had 48
in-sample trades against the pre-registered floor of 60.

## What each role said

- **data-integrity:** NO OBJECTION to the fail; would have BLOCKED a survive.
  Range windows complete on 98.7% of weekdays on both feeds; clock verified
  (no Vantage bars at 17:xx New York). Found the 14-month overlap and 9,732
  flat-filled zero-volume 17:xx bars in the Dukascopy series. *"Any future
  xauduka OOS must be cut at 2025-04-10."*
- **adversary:** SURVIVED (the negative). *"'100th percentile' measures
  session concentration vs all-hours cost bleed, not breakout logic."* Also:
  the 48-trade survivor is under the registered floor; the direction nulls
  settle the registered claim; `orb/30m`'s out-of-sample row is a post-hoc
  look and may not be read.
- **risk:** BLOCK for any promotion (none proposed). Per-trade risk is
  bounded by sizing, not by the stop (signal-bar overshoot is uncapped); no
  spread widening at 08:30/10:00 news in the model. Found that
  `[trading] max_stop_atr, min_reward_risk, cluster_pad_atr, require_basis`
  are read by zero lines of code — the same class of finding as the guards.
- **historian:** the `ny-morning` and `london-open` donchian rows of
  `2026-09-12-gold-intraday-batch-1.md` were near relatives and failed; the
  short-in / long-out reversal is the ICT pattern of the same day. The
  London-range and Asian-range backlog items share the base method and
  should be run only with a disjoint multi-year out-of-sample pre-committed.
- **researcher / execution-realist / portfolio:** not consulted separately;
  their questions are answered above (costs are the venue's; nothing to size).

## What would reopen this

A null with the strategy's own entry window *and* range-sized stops that
`orb/60m` still beats at ≥ 95% on a disjoint four-year sample (cut at
2025-04-10). Nothing about the presets would.

## What this does not say

- It does not say range breakouts never work; it says this one, at these
  costs, on gold five-minute bars, does not pay 1.2.
- It does not say the loop failed: it pre-registered, implemented with
  no-future tests, ran the in-sample, opened the out-of-sample once, and the
  review found a mis-matched control before anything was believed. That is
  the loop doing its job, at a cost of one afternoon.

## Process changes from this pass

1. For a method that only signals in a window, the matched null must be
   gated to that window (`hours:…` in the batch file). Added to the skill.
2. `search` needs `--from/--to` so an out-of-sample market can be cut to
   dates the in-sample feed does not cover. Backlog (infrastructure).
3. The Dukascopy converter should drop flat-filled zero-volume bars.
   Backlog (infrastructure).
4. Config keys nobody reads: enforce or delete. Backlog (infrastructure).
