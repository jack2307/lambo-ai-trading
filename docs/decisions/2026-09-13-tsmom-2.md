# Time-series momentum on gold, sized honestly: beats random holds, not a coin flip on its own trades — closed

**Date:** 2026-09-13
**Question:** Does holding the side of gold's trailing 20/60/120-day return,
rebalanced daily at 18:00 New York and sized at one percent of equity per two
daily ranges, pass the gate on 2018–25 against both a sized random-hold null
and a direction null? (`docs/hypotheses/2026-09-13-tsmom-2.md`, registered at
`53db567`, after `2026-09-13-tsmom.md` was parked on sizing.)
**Outcome:** No, under its own falsifier. The 60-day row replays at PF 2.031 on
75 trades, the 98th percentile of the sized random-hold null, and the 20-day
row at 1.325 on 149, 96th — but with the sides of those same trades permuted
the rows sit at the 86th and 87th percentiles (p = 0.14, 0.13), and the
120-day row at the 55th. The walk-forward has every row inside (93rd–94th at
300 seeds). Three of the 60-day row's 75 trades are all of its profit. The
confirmation window cannot produce 30 trades and was not opened. Closed.

## What was measured

Dukascopy bid, 15-minute bars, 2018-06-16 → 2025-04-10 (160,368 bars), spread
$0.28, no swap. Rebalance at the first bar at or after 18:00 New York, gated
`weekdays` + `hours:1800-1900`; the lookback pinned per row (20/60/120), the
sizing stop pinned (2 daily ranges over 20 days). Nulls: random holds of
half the lookback, entered on the same bars, sized the same (`RandomHold`);
and the method's own trades with each side a coin flip (`null-dir`, rebuilt
in this pass — `9ae323f`). The pass ran three times while three engine
faults were corrected (`2026-09-13-instrument-faults.md`); the numbers below
are from the final run at `9fde7c1`/`9ae323f`, and the earlier receipts are
kept under `runs/2026-09-13-tsmom-2/stop-enforced/` and `flip-replayed/`.

## Reading

Two nulls, two answers, and they are not in conflict. Against random
30-day holds on a tape where gold tripled, the method's trades are in the
top two percent: holding *on the side of the trailing return* did better
than holding on a random side for a fixed month. Against its own 75
intervals with the sides shuffled, it is in the top fourteen percent: with
this few trades and this skew (three trades of +9.4R, +9.3R and +6.3R; 72
others netting −$13 at PF 0.995 — risk's count), a profit factor of 2 is
what one draw in seven of a coin flip produces. The registered falsifier
asked for both at the 95th; the second is the direct test of the claim and
it is not met. The 120-day row, with the highest expectancy of the three,
is a coin flip at the 55th.

## What each role said

- **data-integrity:** VETO — *"no Friday has a bar at or after 18:00: 0 of
  345 Fridays fire. The method rebalances four days a week, not five, and
  the hypothesis's 'each day' is wrong."* Sunday's reopen fires the exit
  and the weekday filter then forbids re-entry until Monday. Point-in-time
  is clean (`bars[..=i]`, `average_day_range` skips today). The confirmation
  is *"a formality"*: warmup is a bar count on the bounded series, 18,720 of
  its 33,589 bars, leaving 130 rebalance bars and ~8 trades at 60 days,
  zero at 120. The primary loses 0.8 years to the same warmup.
- **adversary:** BLOCK — *"the direction null is the one the falsifier
  registers, and it is the only one that isolates the claim … the drift
  null answers a different question — did this beat random 30-day exposure
  to a tape where gold roughly tripled."* Twelve registered looks, twenty-one
  produced across the instrument corrections; *"the final direction null has
  never been exercised on anything but the hypothesis it was built
  against."* To open the window the fixed rows would need PF ≥ 2.607 (60d)
  and ≥ 1.650 (20d).
- **risk:** NO VETO as a paper candidate, VETO past paper — *"the three
  largest trades … sum to $2,664, i.e. 100.5% of net. Strip them and the
  remaining 72 trades net −$13 at PF 0.995."* Worst open excursion −3.37R
  on a trade closed at −0.29R; a $1,097 give-back on the 2019-12-30 long;
  720 days under water after 2020-09. *"Two daily ranges is a
  volatility-scaling unit, not a loss bound"*: March 2020 sized 5.65 lots
  off a pre-crisis range while the day's range was ten units. 27 of 75
  exits (36%) fill on the Sunday reopen bar. *"196 round-turn lots over 6.8
  years — ~29 lots a year on $10k"*: not a rebate generator.
- **historian:** the first record's condition was met (sizing, longer
  window) and the answer is the literature's: a Sharpe of 0.3–0.5 on one
  asset over seven years is not distinguishable from noise at 75 trades.

## What would reopen this

A second asset with seven or more years (Dukascopy silver or EURUSD are
fetchable), a warmup counted in days so the confirmation window exists, the
Friday close and Sunday reopen handled in the method (rebalance at 17:00
Friday, no Sunday exits) — registered as its own claim with those reasons,
not as a re-run. And, on whatever window: the 60-day row's own permuted
null at the 95th, which on this one is PF 2.607.

## What this does not say

- It does not say time-series momentum is absent from gold; it says 75
  trades on one metal cannot show it, and the loop was right to ask for
  two nulls.
- It does not say the sized drift null was wrong; it measures exposure,
  and the method has it.
- It does not say anything about the Vantage 2025–26 window; not opened,
  and not openable at this warmup.
- It says nothing for the volume bot except what the first record said:
  the one method with a literature is a monthly one.
