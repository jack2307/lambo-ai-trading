# Weekend gap fade on gold: the gap does not fill within four hours, at any size, on seven years

**Date:** 2026-09-13
**Question:** Does fading the Sunday-reopen gap (≥ 1 or 2 ATR from the Friday
close, stop one gap beyond the open, target the close, four-hour hold) pay at
Vantage cost on 2018–25? (`docs/hypotheses/2026-09-13-gap-fade.md`, registered
at `53db567`.)
**Outcome:** No. The registered preset replays at PF 0.423 on 207 gaps, the
1st percentile of its count-matched null; the large-gap row 0.768 on 109, 72nd;
the walk-forward cannot select its way out of it (0.36–0.64, 0th–14th).
Direction null 33rd and 66th. Closed on the primary; the confirmation was not
opened.

## What was measured

Dukascopy bid, five-minute bars, bounded 2018-06-16 → 2025-04-10 (481,044
bars), spread $0.28, no swap (measured on the account), maximum hold four
hours (`config/default.toml`, `max_hold_ms`). A gap is a break of more than
24 hours between consecutive bars; the ATR is read at the last bar before the
break so the gap bar's own range does not enter it. Entries gated to
17:00–19:00 New York and the null gated the same way, matched to each row's
trade count. Three rows: the registered preset (1 ATR, stop one gap), large
gaps only (2 ATR), and the 3×3 grid walked forward. Receipts:
`docs/research/runs/2026-09-13-gap-fade/`. This batch ran three times while
the engine was being corrected (see `2026-09-13-instrument-faults.md`); the
numbers below are from the final run at `9fde7c1`, and the earlier ones did
not differ in outcome.

## Reading

At one ATR the fade loses a quarter of its risk per trade and sits below
almost every random entry in the same hour: the Sunday open on a five-minute
bar is not a price to fade, it is the direction the week starts in. Only the
large gaps do better than random, and they do not make money. The four-hour
hold is part of the claim; a gap that fills on Tuesday was never this
method's trade.

## What each role said

Reviews not spawned: the registered row fails the gate on profit factor and
sits at the 1st percentile of its null; no argument was needed to reject it.

## What would reopen this

A hold horizon measured in days rather than hours, registered as its own
claim with the swap cost that a non-swap-free account would pay, and tick
data over the first minutes of the reopen — this project trades neither.

## What this does not say

- It does not say weekend gaps on gold never fill; it says the fill is not
  reachable from the first five-minute bar within four hours at $0.28.
- It does not say anything about the 2025–26 Vantage window; not opened.
