# Doji reversal: a pass on one window, inside the noise once the null was matched, losing on the other two

**Date:** 2026-09-13
**Question:** Asked by the owner: does a doji (body ≤ 5–10% of range, range
≥ 1–1.5 ATR) at the end of a 1.5-ATR move mark a reversal worth fading on
15-minute bars? (`docs/hypotheses/2026-09-13-doji.md`, `-doji-btc.md`,
`-doji-2018.md`, registered at `b801751` and `86d14cd`.)
**Outcome:** No. The strict row passed the primary (2022–25: 61 trades,
PF 1.203, direction null 99th) against a null that turned out to be the
wrong one; matched to its trade count the same row is at the **90th
percentile — inside**. On the confirmation (2025–26) the registered
parameters replay at PF 0.782 on 25 trades; on a never-seen third window
(2018–22, registered before its bars were converted, parameters pinned) PF
0.618 on 121 trades, direction 34th; pooled 2018–2025, PF 0.746 on 188.
BTC fails outright (PF 0.79/0.81, direction 23rd/16th). Closed.

## Reading

Three years, one row, a profit factor three thousandths over the gate on 61
trades: the adversary's arithmetic (binomial p = 0.13; best-of-three at 200
nulls reaches the 95th 14% of the time) already said it was a threshold found
on a sample, and the two looser rows collapsing on the confirmation said
the same — an edge degrades as its filter loosens; a fitted threshold falls
off a cliff. Then the third window, chosen before it was seen, lost on 121
trades. On BTC the fade is at the bottom of its direction null: after a
doji at the end of a move, BTC keeps going.

The night's tally for this pattern is therefore: 2018–22 loses, 2022–25 is
noise, 2025–26 loses. A doji is a bar shape.

## The instrument, corrected

The matched null now calibrates the random-entry rate to the row's own
trade count (`hypotheses.rs::matched_rate`): a control with five times the
trades has a distribution too tight to judge a thin row against. Every
matched-null percentile in earlier records was read against a denser
control; every one of those rows failed the gate regardless, so no verdict
moves, but the percentiles in those tables are optimistic and the
`night-synthesis` record carries the note.

## What each role said

- **adversary:** BLOCK on the survive claim, with the mismatched null, the
  binomial, the cliff between rows and the shortfall against the registered
  sample size; set five conditions for accepting a third-window pass. The
  third window failed condition (c) at the first number.
- **data-integrity / risk / historian:** not re-run; the data and the
  method's exits are the shape reviewed on the ORB pass, and the window is
  described in the table.

## What would reopen this

Nothing about the doji. The one honest positive (direction 99th on 2022–25)
is not repeated on 2018–22 (34th) or 2025–26 (33rd).

## What this does not say

- It does not say candle patterns carry no information at other horizons;
  it says this one, at this cost, on gold and BTC 15-minute bars, does not.
- It does not say the third window was a second bite: it was registered
  with pinned parameters before its data existed locally, and it failed.
