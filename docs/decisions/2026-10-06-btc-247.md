# BTC 24/7 — pre-registration (axis m7)

**Date:** 2026-10-06 (registration; written and committed BEFORE the first run)
**Question:** on a tape with no weekend gap and no session close, does the
`gold-intraday` mechanism set clear the desk's gate on both windows — and is
BTC's per-round cost, in R, already larger than the edge the gate demands?
**Outcome:** PENDING at registration.

## Hypothesis

One sentence: BTC removes the one variable every gold measurement must live
with — the break — so if "crypto 24/7 is easier" is true, the same thirteen
`gold-intraday` constructs should clear `PF >= 1.200` and
`expectancy >= +0.050R` on BTC where they do not on gold; the competing claim
is that BTC's spread, expressed as `spread / stop`, is on its own larger than
+0.050R and therefore closes the question by arithmetic before any PF is read.

## Instruments — named, because they are not the same thing

- `--market=btc`: `BTCUSDT-15m.parquet`, Binance spot tape, `contract_size 1.0
  BTC`, configured `spread = 5.00` USD of price.
- `--market=btcusd`: `BTCUSD-15m.parquet`, Vantage `BTCUSD.sc` CFD,
  `contract_size 0.01`, configured `spread = 17.05` USD of price (MEASURED,
  3.4x the Binance assumption).

Both files exist in `/e/rust/flowdesk/data/bars/` and both cover both windows
(checked before registration: BTCUSDT-15m 70,080 bars 2024-09-12 → 2026-09-12;
BTCUSD-15m 100,798 bars 2023-10-05 → 2026-09-15). Every figure reported will
say which of the two it came from.

## Falsifier — and it can fire

The axis is DEAD, declared, if **either** of these fires:

1. **Arithmetic:** the measured cost per round, read from the run's own
   `cost-matched null: ... cost X% of R` line, is `>= 5.0% of R` on both
   markets. The gate demands `expectancy >= +0.050R = 5.0% of R`, so a cost at
   or above that level means one round's spread eats the entire edge the gate
   asks for, and no 15m mechanism on this instrument at this spread can clear
   it whatever the mechanism is. If this fires, the conclusion stands without
   reading a single PF.
2. **Gate:** 0 of the declared primary cells clear `PF >= 1.200` AND
   `expectancy >= +0.050R` with `>= 40` trades on **both** windows.

The axis is ALIVE only if at least one construct clears both gate legs with
>= 40 trades on window A **and** window B, on the same market.

A result of "this axis opens nothing" is the registered, acceptable outcome.
No cell will be promoted for looking good on one window.

## Multiple-testing count — declared before the first run

- Primary: `--batch=gold-intraday` (13 rows, read from

  running) x 2 markets (`btc`, `btcusd`) x 2 windows = **52 cells**.
- Secondary: the same 13 constructs with the two gold-session filters
  (`weekdays`, `flat:1630-1815`) REMOVED, via
  `--batch-file=config/btc-247.toml`, x 2 markets x 2 windows = **52 cells**.
  This arm exists to measure what the gold filters discard on a 24/7 tape, not
  to find a survivor; it is counted as looks all the same.

**Declared total: 104 cells (run x row).** The multiple-testing ledger at the
end will state the real number beside this one. At 104 cells, noise alone is
expected to put ~5 of them at or above the 95th percentile, which is why the
percentile is not the decision leg here.

## How it will be read

- Gate (not adjustable): `PF >= 1.200` AND `expectancy >= +0.050R`.
- Minimum sample: 40 trades. Below that, nothing is concluded — the record's
  own CRT case gave `PF 1.753 / pct 96 / 14 trades` and
  `PF 0.682 / pct 8 / 178 trades` from the same rule on the same window, and
  the only difference was sample size.
- Both windows required: A `2025-07-01 → 2025-10-01`, B `2025-04-01 →
  2025-07-01`. One window is nothing.
- Every percentile written will carry its `null p50` next to it. The desk's
  null median PF is 0.867, below 1, so a high percentile means "loses less
  than random entry at the same cost, count and side ratio", not "makes money".
- `--exit-mix` is on, so the share of positions closed by the 4-hour
  `max_hold_ms` cap is read before any row is called dead on its merits.

## Known confound, stated before the run

`gold-intraday` carries `weekdays` (Mo–Fr on the New York clock) and
`flat:1630-1815`. On a market that never closes, those two gates discard
roughly two days in seven plus 1h45 a day for a reason that belongs to gold's
session, not to BTC. The primary arm is therefore "the gold mechanism as
written, moved to BTC" and NOT "BTC measured 24/7"; the secondary arm is the
second thing. Both will be labelled as such.

## What would reopen this

A measured BTC spread materially below the configured one (a venue, account or
tier where `spread / stop` falls under ~2% of R), or a mechanism whose stop is
wide enough that the same absolute spread is a small fraction of R — the
break-even spread will be stated so this is checkable rather than rhetorical.

## What this does not say

Nothing here is a claim about BTC on timeframes other than 15m, about holds
longer than the engine's 4-hour cap, or about any instrument other than the
two named files.
