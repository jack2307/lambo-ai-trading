## 1. The four questions, one sentence each

**Q1 — the algebra.** For an exit that is purely STOP or TARGET, write the
threshold win rate of each leg as a function of `reward_risk` (`rr`), and find
the `rr` at which the two legs **swap roles**.

**Q2 — the real `rr` of each mechanism.** Read `[trading] reward_risk` in
`config/default.toml` and **every** override path (per-market, per-batch,
`--params=`), then count mechanisms by which side of the swap point they run
on.

**Q3 — the record against the algebra.** For every distinct printed row in the
`agent/floor-audit` harvest that carries **both** PF and expectancy, check
whether the pair satisfies the Q1 identity. **Rows that do not match are the
result**: the identity holds only for pure STOP/TARGET exits, so a mismatch
says that row carried TIMEOUT / WEEKEND_FLAT / NEWS_FLAT / a strategy exit.

**Q4 — is any row rejected unjustly?** The mirror of `floor-audit` (which found
rows wrongly *admitted* in the 30..39 slot). Count rows that fail **only** the
expectancy leg and rows that fail **only** the PF leg. A non-zero first count
at an `rr` where expectancy is supposed to be redundant means Q1 is missing a
term, and naming that term is the most valuable outcome available on this axis.

## 2. Assumptions the algebra rests on — declared BEFORE the count

**A1** Every trade closes at exactly `-1R` (stop) or `+rr*R` (target). No
TIMEOUT, no WEEKEND_FLAT / NEWS_FLAT, no END_OF_DATA, no trail, no partial, no
strategy-issued exit, no gap through the stop.

**A2** Risk per trade is the same R on every trade, so a mean in R and a ratio
of sums in R are the same statistic.

**A3** Costs are either zero or already inside the two payoffs. The
cost-bearing form is derived separately and its effect on the swap point is
reported, because brief §8 / addendum §E measure costs of **0.85%-38.16% of R**
depending on the stop size.

**A4** PF is the gross ratio `sum(+R) / |sum(-R)|`.

A1 is the assumption that **will** fail on the record, and Q3 exists to measure
how often, not to assume it does not.

## 5. Falsifier — one, stated by the brief that commissioned this axis

**F1 FIRES (the desk's three-leg gate has been a one-leg gate all along) if:**
at **every** `rr` the record actually uses, the PF leg's threshold win rate is
**at or above** the expectancy leg's, **and** the count of rows failing
**only** the expectancy leg is **0**.

If F1 fires, the conclusion to record is that "fails both legs" has been **one
piece of evidence stated twice**, not two.

**F1 does NOT fire** if any mechanism in the record runs at `rr` below the swap
point, or if any row fails the expectancy leg while clearing PF. In that case
the named list of such rows is the deliverable.

## 6. How it is read

For Q1: the two closed forms, their difference in factored form, the
derivative, and the root — written out so a later reader can re-derive it
without running anything.

For Q2: counts of mechanisms by side of the swap point, with the override paths
that exist and the override paths that **structurally cannot** exist stated
separately. "No override is stated" and "no override is possible" are different
findings and are not merged.

For Q3: the residual distribution, and **every** mismatching row **named** with
its printed PF, its printed expectancy, the PF the identity predicts, and the
receipt path. A total alone is not a result (`floor-audit` named all 69 of its
rows).

For Q4: the four cells, and every row in the two single-leg cells **named**.

## 7. What this will NOT answer, declared now

* **Whether any row would pass at a different threshold.** No threshold is
  moved and nothing is re-run.
* **Whether a mismatching row is wrong.** A mismatch says the row did not exit
  purely at stop or target. That is a fact about its exit mix, not a defect.
* **The exit mix of a row whose receipt does not print one.** `--exit-mix` was
  not read by `rescore` until `agent/instr-repair` (brief §1), so for older
  receipts the exit mix is `null`, never 0.
* **Any drawdown figure.** `max_drawdown_*` is not printed by
  `--mode=hypotheses` (addendum §G), so no risk number is promised here.
