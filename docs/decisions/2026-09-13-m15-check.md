# The closed mechanisms on 15-minute bars: the timeframe is not the variable

**Date:** 2026-09-13
**Question:** Asked by the owner: do the opening-range break, the London-range
break, yesterday's-level fade and break (gold), the equity-open break and the
VWAP fade (BTC) look different on 15-minute bars than on five-minute ones?
(`docs/hypotheses/2026-09-13-gold-m15-check.md`, `-btc-m15-check.md`,
registered at `8f2706f`.)
**Outcome:** No. Every row fails the gate on its primary window; the closest
is BTC's equity-open break at PF 1.192 (78th percentile, direction 72nd).
The confirmation windows were not opened. The 5-minute records stand.

## Evidence

Gold, `xauduka:15m` 2022-06 → 2025-04-10 (66,243 bars), nulls gated to each
row's hours:

## Reading

Coarser bars change the trade count and the cost per move, not the
information in the direction: the direction nulls are where they were on
five minutes, and the VWAP fade on 15-minute bars is at the *bottom* of its
direction null — the stretch continues even more clearly. BTC's equity-open
break is again the least bad row (1.19 against a null whose 95th percentile
is 1.51: the New York morning on BTC is wide, noisy, and expensive to be
wrong in), and again short of both the gate and the noise.

## What each role said

Reviews not spawned: a robustness check on closed families with every row
failing; the receipts are the review.

## What would reopen this

Nothing about the timeframe. The families are closed at 5m and 15m; the
skill's rule ("a new mechanism, not a new window") now has two timeframes
of evidence behind it.

## What this does not say

- It does not say 15-minute bars are worse to trade; it says these
  mechanisms carry no direction on them either.
- It does not say anything about 1-hour or daily bars, where the drift
  claims (time-series momentum) live and where the engine's sizing is not
  yet fit to judge.
