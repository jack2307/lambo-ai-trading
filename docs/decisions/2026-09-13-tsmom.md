# Time-series momentum on gold: not testable with this engine's sizing — inconclusive, parked

**Date:** 2026-09-13
**Question:** Does holding the side of gold's trailing 20/60/120-day return,
rebalanced daily, pass the gate on 2022–25? (`docs/hypotheses/2026-09-13-tsmom.md`,
registered at `cae9535`.)
**Outcome:** Inconclusive, and closed for now on that ground rather than on
the numbers. The 60-day row shows PF 2.118 on 26 trades (100th percentile of
the drift null) — under the 30-trade floor; the 20-day row PF 2.153 with an
expectancy of −24R; the 120-day row 7 trades. The direction receipt for the
same 60-day preset shows PF 0.046 over the whole window. Those numbers cannot
all describe one method, and they do not: they describe the engine sizing a
multi-week hold as if its risk were one ATR.

## Reading

`Exits::Strategy` methods carry no stop, so the engine sizes them on an ATR
fallback: one percent of equity over a 14-bar ATR of five-minute bars. A hold
that then runs for weeks realises risk ten to fifty times that, and equity
becomes a path: one early loss shrinks every later lot, one early win inflates
it. Walk-forward folds start from fresh equity and show PF 2; the whole-window
replay shows 0.046 after an early wipe. The "R" column is meaningless for the
same reason. None of this is a fact about time-series momentum.

The drift null is also mis-sized for this shape (holds of half the lookback,
random side), which is why its 95th percentile ranges from 0.97 to 28.

## What each role said

Reviews not spawned: the manager's own reading is that the instrument is
wrong for the question, and no role can judge a number the engine cannot
produce.

## What would reopen this

Volatility-targeted sizing for strategy-managed exits (lots from the
asset's realised volatility over the intended horizon, not from a bar ATR),
a trade-count floor met by a longer window (Dukascopy reaches further back
than 2022 if fetched), and a drift null built for multi-week holds. Then the
claim is worth its record. Registered as infrastructure.

## What this does not say

- It does not say TSMOM fails on gold; it says this engine cannot yet size a
  multi-week hold honestly.
- It does not say the intraday findings are affected: every earlier method
  had a stop and was sized on it.
