--- defect 17, checked PER MARKET: does the risk sizing survive min_lot? ---

raw_lots = (equity x 0.01) / (median stop x contract_size); lots = max(floor(raw/lot_step)*lot_step, min_lot)
(engine.rs:729-730). A clamped market's PF_usd is the PF of a min_lot book, not of a 1%-risk book.

The CRITICAL STOP is the stop above which raw_lots falls below min_lot and the clamp bites:
  critical stop = (equity x 0.01) / (min_lot x contract_size), in the instrument's price units.
A market is not clamped or unclamped: it is clamped ABOVE A STOP SIZE, and that is the number.
